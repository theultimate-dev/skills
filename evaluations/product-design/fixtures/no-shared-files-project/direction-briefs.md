# Direction briefs: Walk detail

Every sketch shows the same content: the walk "Canal loop", 4.2 km, about 55 minutes, three stops (Lock keeper's cottage, Heron bridge, Market steps), a Start walk button, and a note that the towpath floods after heavy rain.

## A: Field guide

Warm paper colours, a serif display face for the walk name, stops as a numbered list with small drawn markers. Calm and bookish.

## B: Transit map

A bold sans-serif, high-contrast colour bands, stops on a vertical line like a transit diagram. Fast to scan.

## C: Night walk

A dark palette with one luminous accent, large type, and generous touch targets for use outdoors at dusk.
