From an idea to kept or removed

Two gates are yours:
approve the bet, then keep or remove it.

01 · agent

Frame

  • reads the context and code
  • two or three options
  • a one-page note: shape, park or stop
→

02 · agent

Shape

  • one bet, ½ day to 2 days
  • checks that say how they are proved
  • no-gos, a keep-or-remove signal
→

Gate 1 · you

Approve

  • the bet
  • what the agent may do alone
  • the commit becomes its pin

03 · agent

Build

  • plan, implement, prove, fresh review, fix, repeat
  • a changelog entry
  • steps for you to try
→

04 · you

Try

  • run the steps it left you
  • judge what needs your eye
  • ask for an in-scope repair
→

Gate 2 · you

Keep or remove

  • Keep: requirements and architecture marked built
  • Remove: code reverted, requirements dropped with your reason

appetite runs out → cut what may be cut, or stop  ·  stopped → reshape or remove
